Fonts
-----
Snapscape uses fonts available on the user's device through a system-font
stack, Georgia, Times New Roman, and generic sans-serif/serif fallbacks.
No font files are bundled or redistributed, and no external font service
is used.

Artwork

The bundled mascot, challenge portraits, sunshine-break and gallery

Generation details are in assets/ARTWORK.md. This notice does not describe
user-selected photos.
